01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I’m a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Melbourne. My research explores intelligent and efficient fronthaul architectures for AI-native RAN, for 5G and beyond.

I’m advised by Prof. Christina Lim, Prof. Ampalavanapillai Nirmalathas, and A/Prof. James Bullock. Before Melbourne, I completed my Bachelor of Engineering (Research and Development) with First Class Honours at ANU.
